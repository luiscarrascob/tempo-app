TEMPO

Support

Tempo is a daily ten-minute indoor bike ride built around songs you know. It's made by one person, Luis Carrasco.

Questions, bugs or ideas? Open a support request (free GitHub account needed). I read everything.

No music playing

Tempo plays songs through the Music app, so you need an Apple Music subscription and to be signed in to it. Check Settings › Apps › Tempo › Media & Apple Music is on.

Heart rate isn't showing

Install Tempo on your Apple Watch (Watch app on your iPhone › Tempo › Install) and open it once to allow Health access. Starting a ride on the phone then starts the watch automatically.

Bobbleheads for new artists

About 90 artists come drawn. For the rest, connect your own OpenAI API key in Settings › Bobbleheads. You need a few dollars of API credit at platform.openai.com (a ChatGPT subscription doesn't include it), and OpenAI asks you to verify your organization once before it draws images. Each new artist costs about 5 to 10 cents, once.

Cadence

Mount your iPhone upright on the handlebars. Tempo reads your cadence from how the bike rocks with each pedal stroke.
