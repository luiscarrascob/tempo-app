TEMPO

Privacy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Tempo is made by one person, Luis Carrasco. It has no servers, no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Nothing you do in Tempo is sent to me.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your rides, settings, weight and age.
  • Health data. Tempo reads your heart rate, weight and age from Apple Health and saves your rides there as workouts. Health data never leaves your device through Tempo and is never used for advertising.
  • Your OpenAI API key, if you add one. It's stored in iCloud Keychain and only sent to OpenAI.

What Tempo sends, and to whom

  • Apple Music. Songs play through Apple's Music app. Song searches go to Apple's iTunes Search API.
  • Wikipedia. To draw an artist's bobblehead, Tempo looks up the artist's name on Wikipedia and downloads their photo.
  • OpenAI. Only if you add your own API key. Tempo sends the artist's name and Wikipedia photo to OpenAI to draw the bobblehead, using your key and billed to your OpenAI account. Nothing about you is included. OpenAI's own privacy policy covers what they do with requests.
  • Tips. Tips go through Apple's in-app purchase system. I don't see who you are.
